## 🚀 ObjectBrain

### Building the foundations of adaptive intelligence.

**ObjectBrain** is my long-term technology and research venture focused on exploring intelligence beyond conventional approaches.

One of our core research directions is **Adaptive Learning Model (ALM)** — an architecture exploring how intelligent systems can continuously:

**Learn → Remember → Adapt → Reason → Improve**

The goal is to explore systems capable of maintaining knowledge over time, adapting to changing environments, and developing more persistent forms of intelligence.
